v2.0.16 (2026-11-11)

Supported units: E180 and C90A build; not yet tested on hardware.

Summary: Fault bits renumbered and compacted: Modbus fault, defrost fault and sensor alarm are removed as
separate bits, and HGBV Timeout is now logged. BMS Modbus input registers and BACnet AI numbers changed:
load FieldServer profile revision 8.00aA (HPC_Firmware bms_gateway/) with this release and remap the BMS
points. A false "HMI disconnected" alarm logged at power-up is fixed. Flash control, iot and the HMI.
