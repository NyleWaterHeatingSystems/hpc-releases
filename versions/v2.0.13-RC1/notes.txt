v2.0.13-RC1 (2026-11-09)

Supported units: E180 and C90A build; neither has been tested on hardware yet.

Summary: Factory page Condenser Flow section: Min Target Flow (the condenser flow loop's minimum, now 1.2 GPM on
all units; E180 was 1.5, C90A 1.0) and Low Flow Alarm (0.7 GPM). The HMI seeds both from control and sends them
back once initialized. Units keep their saved values until Reset Config to Unit Defaults or an edit on the page.
Both HMI-HPC packets grew: update control, iot and the HMI together (the panel warns, both directions). Regenerate
hpc-gateway's conf with --remap. Also: QUICKSTART_SETUP.md and QUICKSTART_UPGRADE.md in hmi_provisioning.
