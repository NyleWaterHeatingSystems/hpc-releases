v2.0.11-RC2 (2026-11-09)

Supported units: E180 (the unit this candidate is being tested on). C90A builds but is untested.

Summary: Alarm bits renumbered with no gaps (faults and warnings; lockouts unchanged), and the BMS reads the full
warnings/faults/lockouts fields at input registers 54-65 instead of Alarm_Reg1-3 (which the data log and MCP still
get in their old layout). BMS Remote drops the heat call and raises BMS Communication Lost when the BMS disconnects.
On the HMI, losing the HPC is a fault (HMI Communication Lost) instead of a blocking popup: unit controls lock and
the unit's readings and setpoints go blank until it is back. USB/SD log storage is all on the Diagnostic page, the
panel offers to install a newer release from a USB drive, and a log drive plugged in at power-up is no longer lost.
Control: idle valve position applies immediately, pump demand works in Off and every demand mode, freeze protection
keeps priority over the resting pump and valve, and Local Stop also applies in MCPLA.

Control, iot and HMI must be updated together (status packet and alarm bits changed). hpc-gateway's conf must be
regenerated with --remap (HMI variable table changed).

Built by tools/create_release.sh: HMI cross-compiled with scripts/build-cross.sh, control and iot with ESP-IDF 5.4.3
and the repo dev signing keys. Not yet run on hardware.
