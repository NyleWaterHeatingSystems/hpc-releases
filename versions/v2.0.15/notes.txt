v2.0.15 (2026-11-09)

Supported units: E180 and C90A build; not yet tested on hardware.

Summary: Low condenser flow between the alarm limit (0.7 GPM) and the flow verification cut-out (5 GPM) now
locks the unit out after 3 failed starts; before, the soft fault repeated forever. The Configuration, Diagnostics
and Factory pages show floats to 1 decimal place. BMS: Alarm_Warning, Alarm_Fault and Alarm_Lockout (input
registers 15-17, BACnet AI 27-29) are removed; use Warnings, Faults and Lockouts (AI 50-52), which include the
HMI's own alarms. Load FieldServer profile revision 7.00aA (HPC_Firmware bms_gateway/) with this release. Flash
control, iot and the HMI.

Changes since v2.0.14:
- [UPDATE] Version 2.0.15 (b4b3325a)
- [UPDATE] Config, Diagnostics and Factory pages show floats to 1 decimal place (25e5cd71)
